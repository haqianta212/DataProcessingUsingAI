# Data Cleaning Assignment – MagangHub Batch 3 2026

This is the assignment for applying to **MagangHub Batch 3 2026** as a **Data Analyst** at X Company. The task is to clean the dataset and find as many problems in it as possible.

I used 3 strategies to do the task:

## Strategies

### 😢 Strategy 1 – Careless AI use
- Handed the whole task over to AI
- Sloppy, unstructured prompts
- Non-premium AI

**What happened:** The AI hit its usage limit, so I was forced to do the cleaning manually with **Power Query**.

**Result:** 4.5 hours of data cleaning, **36 findings** (some of them found by accident).

### 😒 Strategy 2 – Smart prompting, non-premium AI
- Well-crafted, deliberate prompts
- Non-premium AI
- AI used for **data profiling**; cleaning done manually in **Power Query**

**Result:** 3.5 hours, **55 findings**.

### 😃 Strategy 3 – Smart prompting, premium AI
- Well-crafted, deliberate prompts
- Premium AI
- AI used for **both data profiling and data cleaning**

**Result:** 1 hour, **51 findings**, plus a clean, well-organized workbook.

## Comparison

| Strategy | Prompting | AI Tier | AI Used For | Cleaning Method | Time | Findings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 😢 1 | Careless | Non-premium | Everything (until limit hit) | Manual – Power Query | 4.5 h | 36 |
| 😒 2 | Smart | Non-premium | Data profiling | Manual – Power Query | 3.5 h | 55 |
| 😃 3 | Smart | Premium | Profiling + cleaning | AI | 1 h | 51 + tidy workbook |

## Key Takeaways
- **Prompt quality matters more than the tool.** Moving from careless to smart prompting (Strategy 1 → 2) gave the biggest jump in findings: from 36 to 55, while also saving an hour.
- **Premium AI mostly buys speed.** Strategy 3 was 3.5× faster than Strategy 2, with nearly the same number of findings and a neater final workbook.
- **Human involvement still adds value.** The most findings came from Strategy 2, where AI did the profiling but I reviewed and cleaned the data myself.
- **Don't fully hand the work over to AI.** Without clear direction, the AI ran out of quota before finishing, and the work had to start over manually.

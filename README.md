# Data Cleaning Assignment – MagangHub Batch 3 2026

This is the assignment for applying to **MagangHub Batch 3 2026** as a **Data Analyst** at X Company. The task is to clean the dataset and find as many problems in it as possible.

I used 3 strategies to do the task:

## Strategies

### 😢 Strategy 1 – Careless AI use
- Handed the whole task over to AI
- Sloppy, unstructured prompts
- Non-premium AI

**What happened:** The AI hit its usage limit, so I was forced to do the cleaning manually with **Power Query**.

**Result:** 4.5 hours of data cleaning, **36 findings** (some of them found undeliberately).

### 😒 Strategy 2 – Smart prompting, non-premium AI
- Well-crafted, deliberate prompts
- Non-premium AI
- AI used for **data profiling**; cleaning done manually in **Power Query**

**Result:** 3.5 hours, **55 findings**.

### 😃 Strategy 3 – Smart prompting, premium AI
- Well-crafted, deliberate prompts
- Premium AI
- AI used for **both data profiling and data cleaning**

**Result:** 1 hour, **51 findings** (with new findings that 2nd startegy can't acuqire them), plus a clean, well-organized workbook.
